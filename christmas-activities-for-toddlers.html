Christmas activities for toddlers

Simple Christmas ideas for ages 2-5 that need almost no prep: watch a short song video, print a coloring page, count the ornaments, make a card for grandma or color a paper ornament for the tree.

10 easy Christmas activities

  1. Sing the Christmas Tree song and decorate a paper tree.
  2. Count the cookies for Santa (1 to 10).
  3. Freeze dance to the Reindeer song.
  4. Color a free Christmas coloring page.
  5. Trace the numbers on a counting worksheet.
  6. Make a gift tag for every present.
  7. Color and hang a paper ornament.
  8. Play "Who drives this car?" with toy vehicles.
  9. Do the bedtime routine with the Christmas Pajamas song.
  10. Play hide and seek with a toy reindeer.
