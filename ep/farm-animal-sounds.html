Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this farm animal sounds video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Lumo!
  • Today the bus goes to the farm!
  • We will guess animal sounds,
  • and get dressed with Pip!
  • Are you ready? Let's go!
  • Beep beep! The bus is going to the farm!
  • Look! Animals want a ride! Let's count them!
  • Five farm animals on the bus! Let's sing!
  • Let's play a guessing game! Listen to the sound!
  • You guessed them all! Super listening!
  • What a fun day at the farm, friends!
  • We counted animals and got dressed!
  • See you tomorrow for more songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make the animal sounds together, then let your child lead.
  • Point to animals in a picture book and name them.
  • Act like each animal: hop, waddle, stomp or swim.
  • Print the free Farm Animal Sounds coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Farm Animal Sounds” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Farm Animal Sounds” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.
