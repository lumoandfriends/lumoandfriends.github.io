Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this superhero song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Lumo!
  • Today we are super helpers for Santa!
  • Let's get dressed like superheroes! Are you ready?
  • Superheroes collect stars! Let's count!
  • How many stars?
  • Super counting, friends!
  • Look! A magic color machine! Put in a white snowball...
  • Beep beep! What color will come out?
  • So many colors! Magic!
  • Superhero moves! Do it with me, then freeze!
  • You are a super superhero!
  • What a super day!
  • We dressed up, counted stars and made magic colors!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Superhero Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Superhero Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Superhero Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Superhero Song”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 7).
