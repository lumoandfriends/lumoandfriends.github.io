Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this thank you song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Pip!
  • Today is a thank you day! We share yummy food with our friends.
  • Can you say thank you? Thank you!
  • Let's play a guessing game! What food is it?
  • You guessed them all! Great job!
  • Gobble gobble! The turkeys are on the farm. Let's count them!
  • How many turkeys?
  • What does the turkey say? Gobble gobble gobble!
  • Bubbo, what are you thankful for?
  • I am thankful for honey! And for my friends!
  • What are YOU thankful for? Tell a grown-up!
  • We are thankful for you, friend!
  • What a happy thank you day!
  • We guessed foods and counted turkeys!
  • See you tomorrow for more songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Sing the song during the real routine (hand washing, bath or bedtime).
  • Use a sticker chart for each step your child does alone.
  • Let your child teach the routine to a favorite toy.
  • Print the free Thank You Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Thank You Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Thank You Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Thank You Song”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, November 28).
