Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this construction trucks song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Bubbo!
  • Today we are going to build a playground!
  • We need big trucks to help us!
  • Put on your helmet. Let's build!
  • Every truck has a special job. Which truck do we need?
  • Which truck can help?
  • Great job, builders! The trucks are working!
  • Let's build a tower with bricks! Count with me!
  • Ten bricks! What a tall tower!
  • Oh no! Crash! It fell down!
  • That's okay! We can build it again!
  • Look, friends! The playground is ready!
  • Thank you, trucks! Let's play!
  • What a busy day, friends!
  • We built a playground with big trucks!
  • See you tomorrow for more songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Line up toy cars and count them.
  • Make the vehicle sounds: beep, vroom, choo choo, nee-naw.
  • Spot cars, buses and trucks on a walk and name their colors.
  • Print the free Construction Trucks Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Construction Trucks Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Construction Trucks Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.
