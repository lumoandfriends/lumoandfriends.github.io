Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this count the stars & twinkle twinkle video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Lumo!
  • Today we'll sing Twinkle Twinkle Little Star,
  • visit Old MacDonald's farm,
  • and count the stars with me!
  • Are you ready? Let's go!
  • Let's count the stars together!
  • Five little stars! Now let's twinkle our fingers.
  • Open, close, open, close. Twinkle, twinkle!
  • What shape is a star? Can you say star?
  • Star! You are so smart!
  • What color is the star? Yellow!
  • And the moon comes out at night.
  • Can you say moon?
  • Moon! Wonderful!
  • That was so much fun, friends!
  • You sang and counted so well!
  • See you tomorrow for more songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Go on a color or shape hunt around the house.
  • Count snacks, toys or steps out loud together.
  • Ask "what comes next?" and let your child finish the line.
  • Print the free Count the Stars & Twinkle Twinkle coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Count the Stars & Twinkle Twinkle” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Count the Stars & Twinkle Twinkle” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Count the Stars & Twinkle Twinkle”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, November 16).
