Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Go on a color or shape hunt around the house.
  • Count snacks, toys or steps out loud together.
  • Ask "what comes next?" and let your child finish the line.
  • Print the free Opposites Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Opposites Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Opposites Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Opposites Song”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, November 18).
