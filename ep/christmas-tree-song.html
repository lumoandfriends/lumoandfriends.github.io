Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this christmas tree song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Pip!
  • Christmas is coming! Look, we have a big Christmas tree!
  • Let's decorate it together! Are you ready?
  • Let's put balls on the tree! What color is this ball?
  • What color is it?
  • What goes on the very top of the tree?
  • A star! A big shiny star! Our tree is so pretty!
  • Look at all the presents under the tree! Let's count them!
  • How many presents?
  • So many presents! But we wait for Christmas to open them!
  • Brr! It's snowing outside! Let's make a snowman!
  • It is cold! What does the snowman need?
  • Now our snowman is nice and cozy!
  • What a sparkly Christmas tree!
  • We counted presents and made a snowman!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Christmas Tree Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.
