Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Go on a color or shape hunt around the house.
  • Count snacks, toys or steps out loud together.
  • Ask "what comes next?" and let your child finish the line.
  • Print the free Colors Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Is the “Colors Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.
