Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this which hat is mine? video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Whoosh! The wind blew all our hats away!
  • Now the hats are all mixed up!
  • Can you help us find who each hat belongs to?
  • Let's find the right hats! Let's go!
  • Let's play! Who wears this hat?
  • Who wears this hat?
  • You matched all the hats! Great job!
  • Wait! Where's Bubbo? He's hiding!
  • Is he behind the Christmas tree?
  • No! Not there!
  • Is he under the presents?
  • Is he in the big box?
  • Peekaboo! Here I am!
  • We found you, Bubbo! Yay!
  • Let's mix colors to paint our hats!
  • You are a color mixing star!
  • Everyone has the right hat! Song party time!
  • Sing and dance with us!
  • I love the hat song! Again, please!
  • Okay! Sing it with us one more time!
  • We found the crown, the helmet, the cap and the top hat!
  • And we found Bubbo too!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas fun!
  • Bye bye, friends! We love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Which Hat Is Mine? coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Which Hat Is Mine?” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Which Hat Is Mine?” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Which Hat Is Mine?”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 14).
