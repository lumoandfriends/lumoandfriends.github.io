Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this doctor check-up song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Achoo! Hi friends, it's Pip.
  • I have a sniffle today.
  • Let's visit Doctor Lumo! Will you come with me?
  • Let's go!
  • Hello Pip! I am Doctor Lumo. Let's check you up!
  • Can you help me find the right tool?
  • Which tool do we need?
  • You were so brave, Pip! Here is a sticker!
  • We are in the waiting room. Let's count while we wait!
  • Great counting! Now it's our turn!
  • Pip rested and drank water.
  • How does Pip feel now?
  • Happy and healthy! Hooray!
  • Thank you, Doctor Lumo!
  • What a brave day, friends!
  • Remember to wash your hands and rest!
  • See you tomorrow for more songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Sing the song during the real routine (hand washing, bath or bedtime).
  • Use a sticker chart for each step your child does alone.
  • Let your child teach the routine to a favorite toy.
  • Print the free Doctor Check-Up Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Doctor Check-Up Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Doctor Check-Up Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.
