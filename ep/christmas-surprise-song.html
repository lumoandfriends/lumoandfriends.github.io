Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this christmas surprise song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Pip!
  • Look! So many Christmas presents under the tree!
  • What is inside? Let's open them together!
  • Surprise presents! What color is the box?
  • Wow! What a lot of surprises!
  • Let's find shapes on the Christmas tree!
  • Great job! You know your Christmas shapes!
  • When we get a present, what do we say?
  • Thank you!
  • And we can give presents too. Giving makes us happy!
  • Merry Christmas, friends!
  • What a fun surprise day!
  • We opened presents and found Christmas shapes!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Christmas Surprise Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.
