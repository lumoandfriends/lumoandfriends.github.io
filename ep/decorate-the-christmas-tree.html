Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this decorate the christmas tree video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Look! Our Christmas tree is so plain!
  • It has no balls, no lights, and no star!
  • Let's decorate it together!
  • Will you help us? Let's go!
  • It's a color guessing game!
  • What color is it?
  • You know your colors! Great job!
  • Let's count the red balls on our tree!
  • How many red balls?
  • Look! A present under the tree!
  • It's long, red and white, and sweet. What is it?
  • A candy cane! Yummy!
  • Now let's turn on the lights! Three, two, one!
  • Wow! Our tree is so beautiful!
  • Our tree is done! Let's have a song party!
  • Sing and dance with us!
  • I love the decorating song! Again, please!
  • Okay! Sing it with us one more time!
  • We hung balls, lights, a candy cane, and a star!
  • Our Christmas tree is so pretty!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas fun!
  • Bye bye, friends! We love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Decorate the Christmas Tree coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Decorate the Christmas Tree” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Decorate the Christmas Tree” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Decorate the Christmas Tree”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 13).
