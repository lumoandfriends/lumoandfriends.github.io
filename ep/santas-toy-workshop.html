Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this santa's toy workshop video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Oh no! Santa's toys are not ready yet!
  • The elves need help in the toy workshop!
  • Let's be toy makers! Come on, friends!
  • Let's play! Which tool do we need?
  • You know all the tools! Great job!
  • Ouch! I bumped my paw! It hurts!
  • Oh no, Pip! Don't worry. I will help you!
  • Let's open the first aid kit!
  • First, we wash it with clean water.
  • Next, a bandage on the boo-boo.
  • Then something cold, to help it feel better.
  • And last... a big hug!
  • Thank you, Bubbo! I feel better now!
  • If you get hurt, tell a grown-up. They will help you!
  • We made so many toys! Let's count them!
  • Super counting! Santa will be so happy!
  • All the toys are ready for Santa! Song party!
  • Sing and dance with us!
  • I love the toy workshop song! Again!
  • Okay! Sing it one more time with us!
  • We made toys and helped a friend today!
  • Thank you for helping, friends!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas fun!
  • Bye bye, friends! We love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Santa's Toy Workshop coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Santa's Toy Workshop” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Santa's Toy Workshop” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Santa's Toy Workshop”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 16).
