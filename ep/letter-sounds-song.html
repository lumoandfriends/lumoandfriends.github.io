Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this letter sounds (abc phonics) video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Pip!
  • Whoosh! The wind blew away my alphabet letters!
  • Can you help me find them?
  • Let's go on a letter hunt!
  • Look! I found a letter! Say its sound with me.
  • We found six letters! A, B, C, D, E, F!
  • Every letter has a big one and a little one!
  • Great matching, friends!
  • Let's find letters at home!
  • We found all the letters! Thank you, friends!
  • What a fun letter hunt, friends!
  • A is for apple, B is for ball!
  • See you tomorrow for more songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Go on a color or shape hunt around the house.
  • Count snacks, toys or steps out loud together.
  • Ask "what comes next?" and let your child finish the line.
  • Print the free Letter Sounds (ABC Phonics) coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.
