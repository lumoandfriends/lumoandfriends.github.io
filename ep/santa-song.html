Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this santa song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Bubbo!
  • Ho ho ho! Santa is getting ready for Christmas!
  • Let's help Santa today! Can you say ho ho ho?
  • Let's play a Christmas guessing game!
  • You are a Christmas guessing star!
  • Let's put cookies on a plate for Santa! Count with me!
  • How many cookies?
  • And a glass of milk! Santa will be so happy!
  • Shh. It is the night before Christmas.
  • The stars are twinkling. The snow is soft.
  • Close your eyes, little friend. Sweet dreams.
  • Merry Christmas! Good night!
  • What a jolly Christmas day!
  • We guessed riddles and made cookies for Santa!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Santa Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Santa Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Santa Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Santa Song”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 1).
