Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this snow day song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Pip!
  • Look outside! It snowed all night!
  • It's a snow day! Let's go play in the snow!
  • It's cold outside! What do we wear?
  • What's this?
  • Now we are warm and ready! Let's go!
  • Let's make snowballs! Count with me!
  • How many snowballs?
  • Great counting! Snowball fight! Whoosh!
  • Let's find colors in the snow!
  • Wow! So many colors in the snow!
  • What a fun snow day!
  • We built a snowman and found winter colors!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas songs!
  • Bye bye! Stay warm!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Snow Day Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Snow Day Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Snow Day Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Snow Day Song”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 4).
