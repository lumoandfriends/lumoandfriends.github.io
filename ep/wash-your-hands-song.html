Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this wash your hands song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Good morning friends! It's me, Pip!
  • It's a snowy Christmas morning! Let's get ready together!
  • First, let's wash our hands! Are you ready?
  • Bath time! Will it sink or float? You guess!
  • Great guessing, friends!
  • Breakfast time! Let's count yummy fruit!
  • Healthy food makes us strong!
  • Let's wake up our bodies! Copy me!
  • Now you do it!
  • Wow! You are ready for Christmas fun!
  • What a happy Christmas morning!
  • We washed our hands and ate healthy fruit!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Wash Your Hands Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Wash Your Hands Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Wash Your Hands Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Wash Your Hands Song”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 6).
