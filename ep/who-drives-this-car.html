Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this who drives this car? video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Look out the window! So many cars are coming!
  • They are Christmas helpers! They help everyone!
  • Let's find out who drives each car!
  • Let's play! Who drives this?
  • You know all the helpers! Super job!
  • Here come the cars! Let's count them!
  • Great counting, friends!
  • Listen! Which car makes this sound?
  • The helpers delivered all the presents! Song party!
  • Sing and dance with us!
  • I love the car sounds song! Again!
  • Okay! Sing it one more time with us!
  • Thank you, firefighters, police officers, doctors and farmers!
  • You help us every day!
  • See you tomorrow for more Christmas fun!
  • Bye bye, friends! We love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Who Drives This Car? coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Who Drives This Car?” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Who Drives This Car?” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Who Drives This Car?”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 15).
