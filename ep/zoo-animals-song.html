Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this zoo animals song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Lumo!
  • Today the bus goes to the zoo!
  • We will open surprise eggs,
  • and stomp like dinosaurs!
  • Are you ready? Let's go!
  • Beep beep! The bus is going to the zoo!
  • Let's count the animals that hop on the bus!
  • Five animals on the bus! Can you count with me? One, two, three, four, five!
  • Let's sing!
  • Look! Surprise eggs! What is inside?
  • What do you think is inside?
  • So many surprises! Which egg was your favorite?
  • What a fun day at the zoo, friends!
  • We opened surprise eggs and stomped like dinosaurs!
  • See you tomorrow for more songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make the animal sounds together, then let your child lead.
  • Point to animals in a picture book and name them.
  • Act like each animal: hop, waddle, stomp or swim.
  • Print the free Zoo Animals Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.
