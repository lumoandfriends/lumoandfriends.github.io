Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this fall leaves song video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Hi friends! It's me, Bubbo!
  • It's fall! The leaves are falling from the trees!
  • Put on your scarf. Let's play in the leaves!
  • Crunch crunch!
  • Leaves change colors in the fall! Let's look!
  • Red, orange, yellow, brown! So many fall colors!
  • The squirrel is getting ready for winter. Let's help count her acorns!
  • Thank you! Now the squirrel has lots of food!
  • Yawn! Bubbo is so sleepy.
  • Do you know? Some bears sleep all winter long!
  • Do you think Bubbo will sleep all winter?
  • No way! Bubbo wants to play with his friends!
  • What a crunchy fall day, friends!
  • We counted acorns and jumped in the leaves!
  • See you tomorrow for more songs!
  • Bye bye! Love you!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Go on a color or shape hunt around the house.
  • Count snacks, toys or steps out loud together.
  • Ask "what comes next?" and let your child finish the line.
  • Print the free Fall Leaves Song coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Fall Leaves Song” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Fall Leaves Song” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Fall Leaves Song”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, November 27).
