Story and sing-along words

Here is what Lumo, Pip and Bubbo say in this who ate the cookies? video. Read it with your child before or after watching:

  • Oh no! The cookies for Santa are gone!
  • Gone? But Santa comes tonight!
  • Don't worry, friends. We can bake new cookies!
  • First we mix, then we roll, then we bake!
  • Will you bake with us? Let's go!
  • Ding! The cookies are ready!
  • Let's count the star cookies!
  • How many star cookies?
  • Great counting, friends!
  • Wait... Bubbo, what's on your nose?
  • Cookie crumbs! Bubbo, did you eat the cookies?
  • Yes... they were so yummy. I'm sorry!
  • It's okay, Bubbo. Thank you for saying sorry.
  • Let's share! One cookie for each friend!
  • And the rest are for Santa!
  • The cookies are cooling. Let's have a song party!
  • Sing and dance with us!
  • I love the baking song! Can we sing it again?
  • Yes! Sing it with us one more time!
  • We baked, we counted, and we shared!
  • Santa will love our cookies!
  • Tomorrow we decorate the Christmas tree!
  • See you tomorrow, friends! Bye bye!

Activity ideas for parents and teachers

  • Make a paper ornament together and name its color.
  • Count the presents or lights on your tree out loud.
  • Play the song while you decorate, and pause it for a freeze dance.
  • Print the free Who Ate the Cookies? coloring page and talk about the picture while your child colors.

Questions parents ask

What age is “Who Ate the Cookies?” for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. The words are short and simple, and the songs are gentle.

Is the “Who Ate the Cookies?” coloring page free?

Yes. Download the PDF or PNG on this page and print it at home or in your classroom as many times as you like.

Where can I watch “Who Ate the Cookies?”?

Watch it free on the Lumo & Friends YouTube channel or right here on this page (new videos go live at 11 AM New York time on their release day, December 12).
